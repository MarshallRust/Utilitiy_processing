CATALYST UTILITY PROCESSOR - HOW TO USE
=======================================

What it does
------------
Reads the scanned Logan City and Enbridge bills, types the amount, bill
date, service dates and due date into the "Utilities Billed to Tenants"
Google Sheet, and saves a copy of the scan with ENTERED stamped on every
bill that went in.

It only enters bills for a whole property that has its own tab in the
sheet. Bills for one unit (like "61 W 500 N 101") or one utility (like
"61 W 500 N WATER") are skipped on purpose, since they aren't split
between tenants.

The amount it enters is THIS MONTH'S CHARGES ONLY. If a bill also
carries an unpaid balance from an earlier month, that old balance is
left out so it doesn't get split between the tenants again.

It never overwrites anything already in the sheet. If a cell already has
something in it, that bill is listed for you to check by hand.


First time on a computer
------------------------
1. Copy "Catalyst Utility Processor.exe" to the desktop.
2. Double-click it. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click
   "More info" and then "Run anyway". It only asks once.
3. Click "Sign in with Google". A browser tab opens. Sign in with your
   @catalystrentals.com account and click Allow. You can close the tab
   after it says "Signed in". The app remembers you after this.


Every month
-----------
1. Open the bills. Open every Logan City and Enbridge envelope that came
   in the mail. Take out the bills and throw away the return envelopes
   and inserts. Take out any staples so the stack goes through the
   scanner's feeder. Only the first page of each bill matters. Extra
   pages won't hurt anything, they just make it take longer.

2. Scan them to yourself. Put the whole stack in the copier's document
   feeder, choose scan to email, and send it to your own email address.
   Scan it as ONE PDF (not one file per page), in color if the copier
   asks. Crooked or faint pages are the main reason a bill gets missed,
   so straighten the stack before you scan.

3. Save the PDF. Open that email on the computer and download the
   attached PDF. Your Downloads folder is fine.

4. Open the app, click "Browse..." to pick that PDF, and click
   "Read bills". It takes a few seconds per page. Nothing is written
   to the sheet until the very end, after you've checked everything.

5. Step 1 in the app: check the addresses. Every page is listed with
   the address it read, right next to a picture of the address on the
   bill, in three groups:
     Will be entered       - properties we split
     Already in the sheet  - done in an earlier run
     Won't be entered      - everything else, with the reason
   Compare each address with its picture. Everything under "Will be
   entered" should be a property we split, and nothing under "Won't be
   entered" should be. If the app misread an address, fix it in its box
   and press Enter - the line under it shows where it goes now.
   Tick "I've compared every address with its picture", then Continue.

6. Step 2: check each bill. One bill at a time, the page is shown with
   a numbered box around every value it read, and on the right each
   value next to a close-up of that spot:
     1 Total Bill, 2 Bill Date, 3 Service Dates, 4 Due Date
   Compare each value with its close-up. If one's wrong or missing,
   type the right one in its box. Tick "Looks right" for each (fixing a
   value ticks it for you). Total Bill comes already ticked when the
   app checked it against the bill's own math - the green line under
   it shows that check.
   Then "Confirm and next". If a bill shouldn't go in at all, use
   "Skip this bill" and enter it by hand later if needed.
   (Total Bill is this month's charges only. If the bill also shows an
   unpaid balance from before, that's left out on purpose.)

7. Step 3: the app shows exactly what it's about to write. Look it
   over and click "Write ... bill(s) to the sheet".

8. Do the split in Propertyware for every bill that went into the
   sheet. See the next section.

9. Send it to James. Click "Open stamped PDF" in the app and email that
   PDF to James. It has every page you scanned, including the bills
   that weren't split, with ENTERED stamped on the ones that went into
   the sheet. ("Open folder" shows where it's saved, for attaching.)

Anything you fixed or typed in during the review is listed at the bottom
of the run's report under "CHANGED DURING REVIEW", so there's a record
of what the app read and what a person changed.

Running it twice on the same PDF is safe. Bills that are already in the
sheet are listed as "ALREADY IN THE SHEET" and aren't typed in again.


Doing the split in Propertyware
-------------------------------
Do this once for each bill that went into the sheet. The easiest way is
to copy last month's bill for the same account and change the numbers.

Find last month's bill
1. In the search bar at the top of Propertyware, type the company name
   (Enbridge or City of Logan) and click the vendor in the list - the
   one with the truck icon, like "Enbridge Gas".
2. On the left, click "Vendor Ledger".
3. Set Period to "Last Quarter" and click GO.
4. Scroll to the bottom and set "Show rows" to 50.
5. Press Ctrl + F and type the bill's account number. It's at the top
   of the property's tab in the sheet, under "Account Number:", and on
   the paper bill. In Propertyware it shows up in the Ref # column.
6. On the row it finds, click "View". Use a row whose Type is "Bill",
   not "Bill Payment".
7. At the bottom of that bill, click "CLONE".

Fill in the new bill
8. Copy this month's column from the sheet into the cloned bill:
     Bill Date    <- Bill Date
     Due Date     <- Due Date
     Description  <- keep the company name and change the dates to the
                     Service Dates, like:
                     Enbridge Gas - 9/18/2026 - 10/19/2026
     Amount       <- Total Bill (the Amount box under Bill Splits)
   Leave Vendor, Ref#, Terms, Location and Account the way they cloned.

9. Under "Expense Recoveries", click "NEW TENANT CHARGE" once for each
   tenant and fill in the row:
     Lease    - the dropdown is grouped by unit (#1ATTIC, #2EAST, ...).
                Pick the ACTIVE lease under that unit. Ignore the
                Terminated ones.
     Account  - 4101-35 - Utility Reimbursement
     Date     - the bill date
     Amount   - that unit's share from the sheet (the rows under Due
                Date, like "1 - Attic = 20%")
   Skip a unit if the sheet shows 0% for it, or if it has no Active
   lease.

10. Save the bill.


Common messages
---------------
"not a property in the sheet"
    The address doesn't match any tab. Normal for properties we don't
    split. If it SHOULD be split, add a tab named exactly like the
    address on the bill (for example "420 N 400 E") with the same layout
    as the other tabs.

"unit or utility bill, not split"
    Normal - see above.

"the bill's numbers don't add up as read"
    The app double-checks every amount against the bill's total. This
    means it misread a number. In step 2, type the Current Charges
    amount from the bill into Total Bill.

"Enbridge auto-pay bill"
    Auto-pay bills don't print a total or a due date. In step 2, type
    the Current Charges amount and a due date from the bill, or skip
    it.

"couldn't read ..."
    The scan was too faint or crooked. Rescan that page straighter, or
    enter it by hand.

"the sheet already has different values here"
    Something is already in those cells for that month. Check the bill
    against the sheet and fix it by hand.

"2 bills matched this tab"
    Two bills for the same address and company. Enter them by hand.

"no column for Nov" / "no 'Logan City' section on this tab"
    That tab's layout is different from the others. Make it match.

"Your Google account doesn't have edit access to this sheet"
    Ask whoever owns the sheet to share it with you as an Editor.

"Couldn't reach Google Sheets"
    The internet dropped. Wait a minute and run it again. Anything that
    already made it into the sheet is recognized and not typed twice.

Every run is also logged in:
    %APPDATA%\Catalyst Utility Processor\logs
(paste that into the File Explorer address bar)
